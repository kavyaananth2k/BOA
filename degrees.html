REGULATED QUALIFICATIONS FRAMEWORK (RQF)

Qualification Levels Explained

All British Online Academy diplomas sit on the UK's Ofqual RQF framework and provide university credit equivalencies.

LEVEL 3

University Foundation Level

120 Credits | Equivalent to 2 A-Levels

Made up of 6 modules and 6/12 written assignments. Provides entry onto the first year of an undergraduate degree course or Level 4 diploma.

Entry Requirements:
  • Age 16 or above
  • 5+ GCSEs at grades C and above (or Level 2 equivalent)
  • Appropriate standard of English capability
LEVEL 4

First Year Degree Level

120 Credits | Equivalent to 1st Year Bachelor's

Made up of 6/10 modules and 6/10 assignments. Equivalent to 120 university credits and the first year of a UK university degree.

Entry Requirements:
  • Full secondary education & minimum age 18
  • English verification (IELTS/TOEFL or equivalent)
LEVEL 5

Second Year Degree / HND Level

120 Credits | Equivalent to 2nd Year Bachelor's

Made up of 5/9/10 modules and 5/6/7 assignments. Combined with Level 4 (240 credits total), it grants entry to final year BA Top-Up.

Entry Requirements:
  • Completion of Level 4 or equivalent 1st year degree
LEVEL 6

Graduate Level Diploma

120 Credits | Equivalent to Final Year Bachelor's

Made up of 10 modules and 5 written assignments. Upon completion, you are eligible for direct admission onto a postgraduate Master's or MBA course.

Entry Requirements:
  • Age 21+ years old with at least 3 years relevant work experience
  • Hold Level 4/5 qualification or equivalent to first 2 years of a degree
  • Mature candidates with considerable experience considered individually
LEVEL 7

Postgraduate / MBA Pathway Level

120 Credits | Postgraduate Level

Made up of 30 modules and 8 written assignments. Gives entry onto the MBA Top-Up (1 module or 1 module + dissertation) online or on campus.

Entry Requirements:
  • Age 21+ years old with at least 3 years management experience
  • Hold Level 6 qualification or first degree
STRAIGHTFORWARD ASSESSMENT

How Assessment Works at BOA

Assessment at British Online Academy is a structured, stress-free process designed around self-paced learning.

1. Online 30-MCQ Module Assessments

After completing an online module, you complete an online test of 30 multiple-choice questions (1 hour limit, answered in sets of 5). Results display immediately. Unlimited re-sits available within your 5-year access period.

2. Final Written Assignments

When online modules are completed, you take final written assignments provided by BOA to test your understanding. These are externally verified by Ofqual regulated awarding organizations.

3. Receive Ofqual Diploma & University Progression

After successful assignment completion, you receive your regulated diploma and are eligible for university Top-Up progression starting from £6,000.

TOP UP DEGREE OPTIONS

University Progression from £6,000

Our courses are fully recognized by Ofqual and sit on the UK’s Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF), ensuring that your credits are widely accepted by universities in the UK and overseas.
