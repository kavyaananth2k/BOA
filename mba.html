ENTRY CRITERIA

Postgraduate Level 7 Requirements

To enrol on the Level 7 programme, learners must meet the following entry profile:

  • Must be 21+ years old
  • Must have at least 3 years of management experience
  • Expected to hold a Level 6 qualification or a first degree
  • Note: Managers with considerable experience but no formal qualifications may be considered on an individual basis.

UNIVERSITY TOP UP PROGRESSION

MBA Degree Awarding

Upon successful completion of the Level 7 Postgraduate Diploma (120 Ofqual credits), you can progress directly to the final MBA Top-Up stage (1 module or 1 module + dissertation).

Your final MBA degree will be awarded directly to you by the partner UK university you complete your final top-up year with, on campus or distance learning.
