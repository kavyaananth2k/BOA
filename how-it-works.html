LEARNER JOURNEY

Your Online Path to Recognition

Our online business management, hotel and hospitality, and computing courses will help you obtain a diploma, which is recognised by employers and awarded by OFQUAL, the UK government higher education regulating body.

These diplomas can then be used as progression onto completion of a full undergraduate or postgraduate degree course, at an accredited UK or overseas university.

EVALUATION & MARKING

Straightforward Assessment Process

Assessment at British Online Academy is a very straightforward, self-paced process:

Online Module Assessments

After you have completed an online module, you will have an online assessment to complete containing 30 multiple-choice questions. You have 1 hour to complete these (answered in sets of 5) with plenty of time to review before auto-timeout.

Immediate Results & Retakes

Results are displayed immediately. Passing earns a completion certificate. If you don't pass, you receive a "refer" notification with retake guidance. Retake at your own pace with no strict deadlines.

Final Written Assignments

When online modules are complete, you undertake final written assignments testing your depth of knowledge. Successful completion leads to your diploma from an OFQUAL awarding organization.

University Progression Eligibility

You become fully eligible to apply for direct entry into a top-up BA (Hons) degree or MBA through recognised UK universities on campus or via distance learning.
