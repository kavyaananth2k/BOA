How University Progression Works

By completing Ofqual-regulated level 4, 5, 6, or 7 diplomas with British Online Academy, you earn official higher education credits recognised by accepting UK and international universities.

1

BOA Course

Enrol on an online Level 4, 5, 6, or 7 diploma programme and study at your own pace through 24/7 access.

2

Qualification & Credits

Complete module assessments to earn up to 120-240 UK university credits regulated by Ofqual.

3

University Top-Up

Apply for direct entry into the final year BA / BSc or MBA dissertation stage at accepting universities (Top-Up from £6,000).

4

Graduation is awarded directly by the university upon successful completion of your final top-up stage.

Undergraduate & Postgraduate Pathways

Explore how our Ofqual-regulated level qualifications map directly to final-year UK university degree completion.

UNDERGRADUATE PATHWAY

BA (Hons) Top-Up Degree Route

Complete Level 4 & Level 5 Diplomas (240 UK credits total) to enter the 3rd & Final Year of a Bachelor's Degree in Business, Management, IT, or Hospitality.

Key Steps:
  • Step 1: Complete BOA Level 4 Diploma (120 Credits)
  • Step 2: Complete BOA Level 5 Diploma (120 Credits)
  • Step 3: Apply for University Top-Up (Year 3 Final Year)
  • Step 4: Graduate with BA (Hons) from partner UK university
Enquire for BA Top-Up
POSTGRADUATE PATHWAY

MBA Top-Up Degree Route

Complete the Level 7 Postgraduate Diploma in Strategic Management (120 credits) to qualify for direct entry onto the MBA Top-Up dissertation stage (60 credits).

Key Steps:
  • Step 1: Complete BOA Level 7 Postgraduate Diploma (120 Credits)
  • Step 2: Progress to MBA Top-Up Consultancy / Project Stage
  • Step 3: Submit MBA Consultancy Project to UK Partner University
  • Step 4: Graduate with full Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Official Progression Disclaimer

British Online Academy does not guarantee direct or unconditional progression to any university, degree, Master's programme or other course of study. Progression depends on meeting the specific entry requirements, academic standards, English language proficiency, and admissions criteria set by each receiving university. Students are advised to verify progression routes directly with their target institution prior to enrolment.

British Online Academy provides flexible, Ofqual-regulated higher education diploma pathways, enabling learners worldwide to progress towards recognized UK university degrees.
