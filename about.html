WHO WE ARE

Why Choose British Online Academy?

Whether you are looking to enhance your skills, improve your team or pursue a professional education, the British Online Academy’s learning pathways can provide you with all you need.

The British Online Academy delivers online university pathway programmes with full Undergraduate and postgraduate diploma awards to learners from all corners of the world. Learners can fast track their way through to a UK university qualification on campus or by distance learning.

Our web based modules allow students to learn what they want, when they want and how they want and have been designed to facilitate a much faster, more affordable and engaging way to learn. With a 24/7 student networking platform, personal dashboards, 1-2-1 online tutorials, group webinars and bank of pre-recorded group webcasts, the British Online Academy provides students with all the support that they need to succeed.

ACADEMIC GOVERNANCE

QUALITY MONITORING BOARD (QMB)

BOA operates a Quality Monitoring Board to ensure we are always delivering accredited online courses and resources to meet your learning needs. This board meets regularly to assess:

Specific Student Issues

Reviewing individual learner feedback and operational customer service standards.

University Partner Quality

Addressing any quality issues or credit recognition criteria for partner universities.

Assessment Standards

Monitoring marking and assessment standards to preserve award integrity.

Learning Outcomes Alignment

Monitoring material content against applicable qualification learning outcomes.

Operational & Customer Service Quality

Auditing specific operational processes as they affect overall educational delivery.

The Board Members include respected academic and business leaders. Outcomes of reviews are shared online to ensure transparency.

REGULATED QUALIFICATIONS

RECOGNISED PARTNERSHIPS & OFQUAL

All learning materials are designed and written by experienced academic authors, with each module aligned to the relevant learning outcomes and assessment criteria of the applicable qualification.

The Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation (Ofqual) regulates qualifications, examinations and assessments in England. The awarding organizations we work with are recognized and regulated by Ofqual. BOA is an approved Centre for the delivery of nationally regulated qualifications in the UK.
