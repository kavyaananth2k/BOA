ACADEMIC GOVERNANCE

Quality Monitoring Board (QMB)

BOA operates a Quality Monitoring Board to ensure that we are always delivering accredited online courses and resources to meet your learning needs. This board meets regularly to assess:

Specific Student Issues

Directly reviewing student feedback and specific operational issues as they affect quality and customer service.

University Partner Quality

Monitoring any quality issues for university partners and ensuring smooth progression standards.

Assessment Standards

Rigorously monitoring internal marking and external assessment standards for complete integrity.

Learning Outcomes Content Alignment

Monitoring material content against learning outcomes and assessment criteria of applicable qualifications.

Operational & Customer Service Review

Auditing operational processes to ensure all feedback is welcomed so issues can be formally raised via QMB in a professional manner.

The Board Members include respected academic and business leaders. Outcomes of the reviews are shared online to ensure transparency and understanding amongst our partners and students.

UK REGULATED QUALIFICATIONS

Recognised Partnerships

All learning materials are designed and written by experienced academic authors, with each module aligned to the relevant learning outcomes and assessment criteria of the applicable qualification.

BOA works with UK awarding organizations for the delivery of regulated qualifications. The Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation (Ofqual) regulates qualifications, examinations and assessments in England. The awarding organizations we work with are recognized and regulated by Ofqual. BOA is an approved Centre for the delivery of nationally regulated qualifications in the UK.

This helps ensure learners receive a high quality education and, upon successful completion, can achieve nationally regulated qualifications that are widely recognized by universities and employers.

Partner University Qualifications

On completion of the final assignments, BOA students are awarded a recognized diploma qualification. This diploma can be used to top up to a full undergraduate degree or MBA at a variety of UK universities, either on campus or by distance learning.
