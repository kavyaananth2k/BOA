UNIVERSITY TOP-UP OPTIONS

YOUR ONLINE PATHWAY TO A UK DEGREE

Complete a BOA course and university top up from £6,000. Our courses are fully recognized by Ofqual and sit on the UK’s Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF).

Your degree or MBA will be awarded to you by the university you complete your final year with. We currently partner with UK universities who accept Ofqual credits awarded from studying our courses.

British Online Academy does not guarantee direct or unconditional progression to any university, degree, Master’s programme or other course of study. Specific university entry requirements will differ between universities.
